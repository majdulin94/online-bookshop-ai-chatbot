# Original project assets and attribution

The university project's original presentation assets are preserved. They are
third-party material where applicable; inclusion is not a claim of authorship
or a blanket license grant. Missing licensing records alone were not grounds
for removing or replacing an asset.

The exact restored paths, SHA-256 hashes and available provenance are recorded
in [ASSET_INVENTORY.json](ASSET_INVENTORY.json). The 27 static asset paths retain the project presentation, with current owner-supplied
category replacements at `technology.jpg` and `history-politics.jpg`. Existing metadata and
visible credits are retained. Private profile uploads are excluded; the static
default-profile graphic is a project UI asset.

## Current publication media review

The current local artwork, screenshots and 15 raster images embedded in Lottie
animations were visually reviewed. No visible watermarks or private team details
were found in the reviewed publishable assets. No sensitive metadata was found
in the current replacement technology image or new chatbot screenshot.

Current `technology.jpg` and `history-politics.jpg` replace the earlier category
images. Their upstream provenance is not recorded; a visible-watermark review
does not establish a redistribution license. No blanket license grant is made.

Published screenshots are `homepage.png`, `catalogue.png` and `Chatbot.png`.
The deleted cart capture and local supplementary homepage capture/recording
are excluded; see [publication exclusions](PUBLICATION_EXCLUSIONS.json).
